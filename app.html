🌱 Soil Mechanics

• Void Ratio ($e$) & Porosity ($n$): $e = \frac{n}{1-n} \Longleftrightarrow n = \frac{e}{1+e}$

• $S \cdot e = w \cdot G$ ($S = \text{Saturation}, w = \text{Water content}$)

• Darcy's Law: $q = k \cdot i \cdot A$

💧 Hydraulics & SOM

• Hooke's Law: $\sigma = E \cdot \epsilon$

• Flexural formula: $\frac{M}{I} = \frac{\sigma}{y} = \frac{E}{R}$
